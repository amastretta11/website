01 About

I'm a builder and finance operator working at the intersection of technology, capital, and community. By day I advise on Tech M&A at Centerview Partners. By night I think about what it takes to build something people genuinely love.


At Yale I founded Yale Undergraduate Capital Partners — the university's only undergraduate VC club, sourcing deals for firms managing $33B+ AUM. I've spent time at McKinsey and Romulus Capital, and I've always been drawn to the earliest, most uncertain stages of company-building.


Outside the deal table I run creative pop-up experiences under matchatretta — a passion project that started at Yale and turned into a genuine community experiment in bringing founders and builders together over really good matcha.
